Vexillum. One commander, many soldiers. In other words, subagents.

You only talk to one agent. Tell it what you need, and it dispatches the work for you.

Each soldier gets its own camp, which is just a git worktree. So nobody steps on your work, or on each other.

Meanwhile, the sentinel keeps watch. It wakes the commander only when a soldier finishes, or when it gets blocked and needs a decision.

And nothing reaches your history without your approval. Land it, to merge locally. Or ship it, to run the tribunal and open a real pull request.

Talk to one. They all work. Vexillum.
